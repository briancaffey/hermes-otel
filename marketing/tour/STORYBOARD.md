# STORYBOARD.md — hermes-otel tour

Nineteen scenes, one sub-composition each (`compositions/sNN.html`), generated by
`tools/build_comp.py` from the narration cues in `assets/voice/cues.json`. Scene lengths follow the
voice: 1.1 s lead, the sentence block, 0.9 s tail, 0.5 s push. The two terminal scenes last as long
as their clips when the clips run longer than the narration. Consecutive scenes overlap by the push
on alternating tracks (1, 2); the terminal clips are `<video>` elements on tracks 3 and 4 at the
root of `index.html` (HyperFrames only drives media there), narration sits on track 10. Total ≈ 352 s.
