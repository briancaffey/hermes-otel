# STORYLINES.md — three films, one picked

Brief: a sub-90 s narrated demo for the Hermes Agent Plugin Hackathon (Nous Research), posted on X
tagging @NousResearch. Judges score usefulness, reliability, polish and creativity, and want to "see
the plugin working". Style source: Nous / Hermes Agent's own materials — classical engraving
treated as print (dither, halftone, duotone), electric blue `#0000f2` on paper white `#f2f2f2`, tall
condensed caps (Rules), a tracked mono kicker over a short sentence-case line (the Hermes Desktop
film's caption grammar), and the Hermes Teal dashboard (`#041c1c` / `#ffe6cb`).

## A — "The Tracks" (picked)

**Device:** a myth sets the need. **Form:** object-to-object chain between two worlds.

On the day he was born, Hermes stole Apollo's cattle and walked them backwards so nobody could
follow the tracks (Homeric Hymn to Hermes). Agents do the same: a question in, an answer out, the
middle gone. The hoofprints become span bars, the bars become the real waterfall, and the trace's
thread fans out to seven real backends, the way the many-armed Hermes of the Hermes Agent site holds
every string. Hermes then reads its own tracks in the terminal. Ends back in the engraving world on
the caduceus and the install command: "Follow the messenger."

- First 2 s: the Goltzius *Mercurius* engraving in electric-blue duotone fills the right of the frame,
  cloven hoofprints stamp across the paper toward him, pointing the wrong way.
- Beats: myth → black box → reveal → the trace → the dashboard → every thread / every backend →
  the agent reads its own tracks → private by default → end card.
- Look: two worlds, one palette system — engraving print world (blue on paper) and Hermes Teal
  product world; Rules Compressed caps, JetBrains Mono kickers, Collapse captions.
- Sound: a minimal pulse under the myth, the music opens on the reveal, cuts on the beat through the
  backend roll, settles under the end card. Stamps for the hoofprints, ticks on the span bars, one
  riser into the backend roll, one impact on the lockup.

Why first: it is the only one with a story a stranger remembers, it is native to Nous's mythic
register, and it still puts the real plugin on screen by ~15 s and keeps it there for 60 s.

## B — "One continuous take"

**Device:** the result first. **Form:** an endless zoom.

Opens on a finished Grafana trace, then dives into one span bar, which opens into the Hermes
dashboard showing the same turn, then into one tool row, which opens into the terminal where the
agent ran it, then out through the terminal's span tree into the wall of 27 backends. No cuts; every
world is inside a detail of the last. Look: Hermes Teal only, glass-free, dense. Sound: one
continuous build, 110 bpm, no voice gaps. Strong on polish, weaker on story; risks feeling like a
screen-recording montage.

## C — "Type carries the argument" (the bold one)

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat.

Giant condensed caps in electric blue on paper slam on the beat — "WHAT DID YOUR AGENT DO?" —
interleaved with half-second flashes of real screens, dithered to two colours, building to a
full-frame span tree. Almost no narration: kicker lines and music. Very Nous-poster, very shareable
on X, but says less about how the plugin works and leans on music we don't have yet.
